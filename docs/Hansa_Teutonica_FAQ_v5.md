# Hansa Teutonica FAQ (Version 5)

### Question

Does using **Swap Kontor** to move a Kontor onto a gold coin office award the prestige point?

**Answer.**
No. Prestige points from a gold coin are awarded only when a Kontor is established on that office, not when an existing Kontor is moved there by **Swap Kontor**.

### Question

Can an Extra Kontor be moved using **Swap Kontor**?

**Answer.**
No. Extra Kontors cannot be involved in **Swap Kontor** actions.

### Question

Can the **Swap Kontor** bonus marker swap VP spots in Coellen?

**Answer.**
No. VP spots in Coellen are not Kontors.

### Question (v5)

Can **Move 3 Tradesmen** remove any combination of Traders and Merchants from trade routes, or do Merchants count as two resources?

**Answer.**
It may remove any combination of 3 Traders and/or Merchants from trade routes, including 2 Merchants and 1 Trader.

### Question

If reaching 20 prestige points occurs during **Establish a Trade Route**, does the game end immediately?

**Answer.**
No. Complete the entire **Establish a Trade Route** action, including all of its effects. The game ends after the action is complete, not during it.

### Question

If taking replacement bonus markers empties the supply, do those replacement bonus markers count for final scoring?

**Answer.**
No. Replacement bonus markers do not count for prestige points and are discarded before final scoring. The game ends after the current **Establish a Trade Route** action, when a replacement bonus marker cannot be taken from the supply.

## Final Scoring (Evaluation Of Prestige Points)

### Question

In the end game when you score networks, do you score all your cities if they are connected or do you just pick one city as a hub and score all the spokes (trade routes) connected to it?

**Answer by Designer Andreas Steding.** You may select one network of connected Kontors. If you have two networks (not connected to each other) you may only score one of them. Btw you count your Kontors! Thus two Kontors in one city count as two.
